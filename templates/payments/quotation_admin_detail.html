View: payments.views.quotation_detail_admin Context: quotation (with request, request.customer and labour_estimate), other (the other quotation of the same request: material for a labour quotation and the other way round, or None) and request_payment (payments.models.request_payment_state: none, unpaid, partial, paid). Material quotation only: receipts (the handyman's MaterialReceipt list), used for the "Waiting for Receipts" / "View Receipts" button. Payment comes after BOTH quotations are accepted, and Schedule Appointment after both are paid. Works for labour and material quotations. A material quotation goes back to Material Quotations (payments:admin_materials) and lights up that sidebar item. Material flow (FR-A-9): the customer approves the material quotation, the handyman buys the materials and uploads the receipts, and the material quotation is paid like the service quotation. Once both are paid, Schedule Appointment shows. - Item list: the lines of work_description (there are no line items, quantities or unit costs). - Margin: total_amount minus labour_estimate.amount, worked out in the browser. - Activity: built from created_at, revision_number, responded_at, status and payment (there is no activity log, so "sent" has no date). - Buttons follow the model rules: Send / Re-issue (draft, revised), Edit / Revise (draft, revised, revision_requested), Cancel (not cancelled, declined or paid), Record payment (ready_for_payment). - Customer response prompt (accepted, revision_requested, declined): opens by itself once per answer in this browser (localStorage), and from "View response". There is no "closed" status or decline reason in the backend, so "Mark as Closed" only stops the prompt opening again. Schedule Appointment opens the Schedule Service Appointment page (accounts_admin:appointment_schedule). - Revision (FR-A-9): when the customer asks for changes, the admin sends them to the handyman, the handyman updates the estimate, then the admin revises and re-issues. BACKEND: there is no way to send an approved estimate (or a forwarded material request) back to the handyman yet, so "Ask Handyman to Update" is greyed out, and Revise is locked while the customer's changes wait for the handyman (so the handyman step cannot be skipped).
